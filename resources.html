Crisis & helplines

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988 (24/7).
  • 911 — medical or safety emergencies.
  • Milwaukee County 24-Hour Crisis Line — (414) 257-7222 (all ages, 24/7; can dispatch Mobile Crisis).
  • Waukesha County 24-Hour Crisis Line — (262) 547-3388 (24/7). Mobile crisis teams can come to you, and the Crisis Resource Center at 1501 Airport Rd, Waukesha takes walk-ins 8am–8pm daily (referrals (262) 896-8589).
  • IMPACT 211 — call 211, or text your ZIP to 898211 (24/7 connection to local help).
  • Crisis Text Line — text HOPELINE to 741741 (24/7).
  • Full crisis details are on our Get Help Now page.

Therapist spotlight

Getting to know the people behind the care.

Lauren Miller, LPC-IT

Photo to add.

Lauren is a Licensed Professional Counselor In-Training (LPC-IT) who supports adults and couples experiencing anxiety, depression, grief, life transitions, postpartum/prenatal challenges, stress, and trauma. Lauren anchors her practice in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Person-Centered Therapy techniques, but she also draws on multiple therapeutic methods to best suit the person and situation.

In this space, authenticity and empathy are always held in high regard. Lauren strives to create, and maintain, a compassionate, safe, and non-judgmental space where you can work toward clarity, healing, and the truest version of yourself; without pressure or predetermined paths.

Lauren earned her Master of Science in Professional Counseling from Mount Mary University, with an emphasis in Clinical Mental Health.

University training clinic offering therapy on an income-based sliding scale. Confirm current details.

Wisconsin DHS — Mental Health

State hub with a county-by-county provider directory and program information.

Open Path Psychotherapy Collective

For when therapy isn't affordable. A nonprofit network of therapists offering sliding-scale sessions — a one-time $65 lifetime membership, then individual sessions at $40–70 ($30 with a student intern) and couples or family sessions at $40–80. Open to people without insurance, or whose insurance doesn't adequately cover therapy.

Empowerment Within — Brainspotting

A featured clinic resource for brainspotting — a focused therapy approach that helps people process trauma and distress. Contact details and link to confirm.

Resources by area

Many organizations above serve the whole region — here's a quick guide by location.

Milwaukee County

Milwaukee County Behavioral Health (crisis line (414) 257-7222), IMPACT 211 (dial 211), the Milwaukee Warmline, NAMI Southeast Wisconsin, and the community health centers above all serve Milwaukee County.

Waukesha County & families

Waukesha County Health & Human Services runs a 24-hour crisis line at (262) 547-3388, mobile crisis teams that come to you, and a walk-in Crisis Resource Center (1501 Airport Rd, Waukesha, 8am–8pm daily). NAMI Southeast Wisconsin also covers Waukesha County with free support groups and family programs (office (262) 524-8886).

Hales Corners & nearby suburbs

Hales Corners residents are served by the Milwaukee County services above and nearby providers, including Ellie Mental Health. Dial 211 for the closest match to your needs. Specific Hales Corners programs to add.
